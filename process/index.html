ATELIER CRAFTSMANSHIP

Our Process — Handcrafted Artisanal Atelier

Every piece is slow-crafted loop by loop in our coastal studio. We reject mass production in favor of individual fitting and heirloom quality.

STEP 01

Ethical Yarn Sourcing

We select fast-drying unbleached linen, organic long-staple cotton, and soft bamboo fibers. Every batch is hand-inspected for tensile strength, sun resistance, and skin softness.

STEP 02

Loop-by-Loop Knotting

Using wooden hooks, master knitters execute tension-controlled stitches. Up to 18 hours of concentrated handwork go into constructing a single bikini set.

STEP 03

Anatomical Body Shaping

Dual sizing and custom fitting calculations are applied during the stitching process to ensure cup support and waist hold without harsh pinching.

STEP 04

Lining & Elastic Reinforcement

Every piece is hand-lined with quick-dry matte swim jersey and finished with covered elastic edges to retain shape wet or dry.

STEP 05

Final Quality Inspection & Packaging

Each completed item is fit-checked, steam-softened, and wrapped in organic cotton dust covers before dispatching from our studio.
