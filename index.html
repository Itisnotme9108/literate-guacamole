Slow Craftsmanship

The Atelier Knotted Process

Five meticulous steps from unspun natural fibers to your finished resortwear piece.

01

Yarn Selection

Sourcing organic linen, fast-drying cotton, and hypoallergenic bamboo threads.

02

Hand Knotting

Loop-by-loop hand stitching for structural tension and soft stretch memory.

03

Silhouette Shaping

Sculpting halter lines, scalloped hems, and high-cut skimpy bottoms to proportion.

04

Soft Elastic Lining

Reinforcing interior edges with subtle elastic bands to preserve shape in ocean water.

05

Final Quality Inspection

Inspecting tension, hardware security, and finishing stitches before atelier dispatch.
